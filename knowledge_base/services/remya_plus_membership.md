# Remya Plus Membership Guide

Remya Plus is Remya Industries Ltd's paid membership. Last updated: 01 October 2026.

## Plans and benefits

### What does Remya Plus cost and what do I get?
Remya Plus costs Rs. 999 per year or Rs. 129 per month. Members get free express delivery on eligible orders in metro cities, free Remya Fresh grocery delivery on orders of Rs. 99 or more, and early access to sales.

## Renewal

### How does auto-renewal work?
Memberships renew automatically on the renewal date using the saved payment method. A reminder is sent by email and SMS 3 days before every renewal. Auto-renewal can be switched off any time from Account > Remya Plus > Manage.

### I was charged for a renewal I did not want. Can I get a refund?
If you cancel within 7 days of the renewal charge and have not used any Plus benefit since the renewal, the renewal amount is refunded in full to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. If a Plus benefit has been used since the renewal, the membership continues until the end of the paid period and is not refunded.

## Cancellation

### How do I cancel Remya Plus?
Go to Account > Remya Plus > Cancel Membership. Your benefits continue until the end of the current paid period unless you qualify for a renewal refund, in which case the membership ends when the refund is issued.

## Benefit problems

### I am a Plus member but was charged for delivery.
Free express delivery applies only to eligible orders in metro cities, and free Remya Fresh delivery only to grocery orders of Rs. 99 or more. If an eligible order was charged a delivery fee, report it from My Orders > Help and the fee is refunded within 2 business days.
