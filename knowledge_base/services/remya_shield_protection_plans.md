# Remya Shield Protection Plans Guide

Remya Shield offers Extended Warranty and Device Protection plans for electronics and appliances bought on Remya Industries Ltd. Last updated: 01 October 2026.

## Plans

### What do Remya Shield plans cover?
- Extended Warranty: extends the manufacturer warranty by 1 or 2 years and covers the same manufacturing defects.
- Device Protection (phones, laptops, tablets): covers accidental damage, liquid damage and screen damage for 1 year.
Theft, loss and cosmetic scratches are not covered by any plan.

### When can I buy a Remya Shield plan?
A plan can be bought with the product or within 30 days of the product's delivery, from My Orders > Add Remya Shield.

## Claims

### How do I raise a Remya Shield claim?
1. Go to My Orders, select the product and tap Remya Shield > Raise Claim.
2. Describe what happened and upload photos of the damage.
3. A free pickup or technician inspection is scheduled within 3 business days.
4. The claim decision is shared within 7 business days of the inspection, and approved claims are repaired or, if repair is not possible, replaced.
Device Protection claims have a deductible of Rs. 499 per accidental or screen damage claim, and up to 2 claims can be made per plan year.

### My Remya Shield claim was rejected. What can I do?
You can appeal a rejected claim within 15 days from My Orders > Remya Shield > Appeal. Appeals are reviewed by a human claims specialist, who contacts you within 2 business days.

## Cancellation

### Can I cancel my Remya Shield plan?
A plan cancelled within 15 days of purchase is refunded in full if no claim has been made. After 15 days, plans cannot be cancelled.
