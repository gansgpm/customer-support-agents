# Remya Care Installation & Repair Service Guide

Remya Care is Remya Industries Ltd's installation, demo and repair service. Last updated: 01 October 2026.

## Installation

### Which products get free installation and how soon?
Free installation is provided for air conditioners, washing machines, televisions and water purifiers within 48 hours of delivery in metro cities and within 4 business days elsewhere. Installation is booked automatically after delivery and can be rescheduled from My Orders > Schedule Installation.

### What is included in installation, and what costs extra?
Standard installation includes fixing the unit, connecting it to existing water and power points, and a demo of the main features. Extra materials are charged as per the Remya Care rate card shown before the visit, for example additional AC copper piping beyond 3 metres at Rs. 450 per metre, or a TV wall-mount bracket if you do not have one.

## Technician visits

### The technician did not come for my installation or repair visit.
Technician visits are scheduled in 2-hour windows and you receive the technician's name and phone number on the morning of the visit. If the technician does not arrive within the window, report it from My Orders > Help: the visit is rescheduled on priority within 24 hours and a Rs. 100 Remya Industries Ltd Wallet credit is added for the missed visit.

### How do I reschedule or cancel a technician visit?
Reschedule or cancel from My Orders > Schedule Installation at least 4 hours before the visit window, at no cost.

## Repairs

### How do I book a repair for an appliance or device?
Book a repair from Account > Remya Care > Book a Repair. For products under manufacturer warranty or a Remya Shield plan, the visit and covered parts are free. For out-of-warranty products, a visit charge of Rs. 299 applies and is adjusted in the final repair bill if you go ahead with the repair. Parts are charged at the brand's price list, and Remya Care repairs carry a 30-day service warranty.

## Service complaints

### The technician damaged my property, was rude or asked for extra money.
Remya Care technicians must not collect any payment outside the Remya Industries Ltd app. Complaints about technician conduct, property damage or payment demands are escalated to the Remya Care quality team, who contact you within 4 hours and complete an assessment within 3 business days.
