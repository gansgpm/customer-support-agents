# Remya Fresh Grocery Support Guide

Covers fruits and vegetables, dairy, bakery, meat and fish, staples and daily essentials ordered through Remya Fresh. Last updated: 01 October 2026.

## Delivery slots

### How does Remya Fresh delivery work?
Remya Fresh delivers in 2-hour slots between 7:00 AM and 10:00 PM in metro cities. Delivery is free for orders of Rs. 199 or more; smaller orders have a delivery fee of Rs. 30. Slots can be changed from My Orders until the order is packed.

### My grocery order did not arrive in the selected slot.
If the delivery partner misses the slot, the order is delivered in the next available slot on priority and the delivery fee, if any, is refunded automatically. You can also cancel the order for a full refund from My Orders once the slot has passed.

## Freshness and quality

### My milk, vegetables, fruit or other items were spoiled, rotten, expired or damaged.
Perishable items cannot be returned, but quality issues are refunded without a return. Report the item within 24 hours of delivery from My Orders > Report an Issue with a photo of the item and its packaging. The refund for the affected item is credited to your Remya Industries Ltd Wallet instantly, or to the original payment method within 2 business days if you choose that option.

### An item is missing from my grocery order.
Report the missing item within 24 hours of delivery from My Orders > Report an Issue. Missing grocery items are refunded within 2 business days after the packing record is checked.

## Substitutions

### I received a different item than I ordered.
When an item is unavailable, Remya Fresh may substitute a similar item of the same or lower price only if you allowed substitutions at checkout. If you did not allow substitutions or do not want the substitute, report it within 24 hours for a refund.

## Food safety

### I found a foreign object in a food item or felt unwell after eating it.
Keep the item and its packaging and do not consume it further. If you feel unwell, please consult a doctor. Food safety concerns are escalated to the Remya Fresh Quality team, who contact you within 1 hour during Remya Fresh hours and arrange collection of the item for investigation.
