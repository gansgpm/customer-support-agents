# Electronics Support Guide

Covers mobile phones, laptops, tablets, televisions, headphones and speakers, smartwatches, cameras and accessories such as chargers and power banks. Last updated: 01 October 2026.

## Returns and replacements for electronics

### Can I return a phone, laptop or other electronic product?
Electronics can be returned within 10 days of delivery only if they are defective or damaged; a replacement is offered first and a refund only if a replacement is not available. Change-of-mind returns are not accepted for electronics. Devices must be returned with the box, charger, cables, manuals and free gifts.

### My new phone or laptop is dead or does not switch on.
If the device does not power on at all within 48 hours of delivery, report it from My Orders > Report an Issue as Dead on Arrival. A Dead on Arrival device is picked up within 2 business days and replaced without a technician check. Charge the device for at least 30 minutes with the original charger before reporting.

### What should I do before sending a device for replacement or repair?
Back up your data, sign out of your accounts (Google, Apple ID, Samsung account) and factory reset the device. Remya Industries Ltd is not responsible for data left on returned devices, and devices that are still locked to an account cannot be processed.

## Warranty

### What warranty comes with electronics?
Mobile phones, laptops, tablets, televisions and other electronics come with a 1-year manufacturer warranty. Accessories such as chargers, cables and earphones have a 6-month warranty. After the 10-day return window, warranty repairs are handled by the brand's authorised service centre using the invoice from My Orders.

## Accessories

### The charger, cable or earphones are missing from my phone box.
Report the missing accessory within 48 hours of delivery from My Orders > Report an Issue with a photo of the opened box and the IMEI label. The missing accessory is shipped within 3 business days after verification, or its value is refunded.

## Battery and safety

### My phone or laptop battery is swelling or the device is overheating.
Stop using and charging the device immediately, unplug it, and keep it away from flammable materials. Do not try to open or puncture the battery. Battery swelling and overheating are safety issues: the case is escalated to an Electronics safety specialist who contacts you within 2 hours during support hours and arranges a free pickup for inspection.

## Software and setup

### My device is slow, hangs or an app is not working.
Install the latest software update, restart the device and free up at least 10% of the storage. If the problem continues, the brand's authorised service centre can diagnose software faults under warranty.

### Do you install televisions?
Television wall-mounting and installation are provided by Remya Care Installation & Repair; see the Remya Care guide for timelines.
