# Home & Kitchen Appliances Support Guide

Covers large appliances (air conditioners, washing machines, refrigerators, water purifiers, geysers) and small kitchen appliances (blenders, mixer grinders, air fryers, kettles, toasters, irons). Last updated: 01 October 2026.

## Damaged or defective appliances

### My blender, mixer, kettle or other small appliance is broken. Can I get a replacement?
Yes. Report it within 48 hours of delivery for physical damage, or within 10 days of delivery for a manufacturing defect, from My Orders > Report an Issue, with photos or a short video of the problem. A free pickup is scheduled within 2 business days and the replacement is dispatched within 3 to 5 business days after pickup. If the same model is out of stock, you receive a full refund.

### My air conditioner, washing machine or refrigerator is not working after delivery.
Do not try to repair or open the appliance, because unauthorised repairs void the warranty. Report it from My Orders > Report an Issue; a technician visit is scheduled within 2 business days to verify the issue before a replacement or repair is approved.

## Warranty

### What warranty do appliances come with?
- Kitchen appliances (blenders, mixer grinders, air fryers, kettles): 2-year manufacturer warranty on the motor and 1 year on other parts.
- Air conditioners and refrigerators: 1-year comprehensive warranty and 5 years on the compressor.
- Washing machines: 2-year comprehensive warranty and 10 years on the motor.
Warranty service after the return window is provided by the brand's authorised service centre or by Remya Care for brands listed as Remya Care partners.

## Installation

### Is installation included for appliances?
Free installation is included for air conditioners, washing machines and water purifiers, and is booked through Remya Care Installation & Repair. Small kitchen appliances do not need installation.

## Safety

### I got an electric shock, smelled burning or saw sparks or smoke from an appliance.
Switch off the power at the socket or main switch immediately and do not use the appliance again. If there is a gas smell, open the windows and do not operate electrical switches. Appliance safety issues are escalated to an Appliances safety specialist who contacts you within 2 hours during support hours and schedules a technician visit within 24 hours.
