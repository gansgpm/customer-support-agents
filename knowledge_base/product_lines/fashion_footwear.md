# Fashion & Footwear Support Guide

Covers shoes, sandals and other footwear, clothing and fashion accessories such as bags and belts. Last updated: 01 October 2026.

## Returns and exchanges

### How long do I have to return or exchange shoes and clothes?
Footwear, apparel and fashion accessories can be returned or exchanged within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unused, unwashed and have their original tags and shoe box. Innerwear and personalised items cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or defective. Returns are started from My Orders > Return, and a free pickup is scheduled within 2 business days.

### Can I exchange for a different size or colour?
Yes. Choose Exchange on the return screen and select the new size or colour. The new item is dispatched when the original item is picked up, subject to stock. Size exchanges are free within the 30-day window.

## Sizing

### How do I choose the right shoe or clothing size?
Each product page has a size chart in UK, US and EU sizes for footwear, and chest, waist and length measurements in centimetres for clothing. If you are between sizes, choose the larger size.

## Quality

### The colour looks different from the photo.
Colours can look slightly different because of screen settings and studio lighting. If the colour is clearly different from the product description, you can return or exchange the item within 30 days.

### My shoe sole came off or the stitching opened.
Footwear has a 90-day warranty against sole separation and manufacturing defects. Report it from My Orders > Report an Issue with photos; the pair is replaced or refunded after a quality check. Normal wear and tear is not covered.

### How should I wash my clothes?
Follow the care label on the garment. Items damaged by washing against the care label instructions cannot be returned.
