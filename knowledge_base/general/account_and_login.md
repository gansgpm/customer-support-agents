# Remya Industries Ltd Account & Login Help

Last updated: 01 October 2026.

## Password reset

### I forgot my password and cannot log in. How do I reset it?
1. Open the Remya Industries Ltd app or website and tap Login.
2. Tap Forgot Password.
3. Enter your registered email address or mobile number.
4. Enter the 6-digit OTP sent to you. The OTP is valid for 10 minutes.
5. Set a new password with at least 8 characters, including one number and one special character.
6. Log in with the new password. You are logged out of all other devices for your security.

### I am not receiving the OTP.
Wait 2 minutes before requesting a new OTP, check the spam or promotions folder for email OTPs, and make sure your phone has network coverage and DND is not blocking transactional SMS. You can request an OTP up to 3 times per hour. If you still do not receive it, contact support with your registered email or mobile number.

## Locked accounts

### My account is locked.
For security, an account is locked for 30 minutes after 5 wrong password attempts. Wait 30 minutes and log in again, or reset your password using Forgot Password to unlock it immediately.

## Updating account details

### How do I change my email address or mobile number?
Go to Account > Profile > Edit. Changing your email or mobile number requires an OTP sent to both the old and the new contact. If you no longer have access to the old number, contact support with a photo ID for manual verification.

### How do I delete my account?
Go to Account > Settings > Delete Account. Deletion is completed within 7 days. Orders that are still in progress must be delivered or cancelled first.

## Suspicious activity

### Someone else logged in to my account or placed orders I did not make.
Change your password immediately using Forgot Password and log out of all devices from Account > Security. Do not share OTPs with anyone; Remya Industries Ltd staff will never ask for your OTP or password. Unauthorised orders and suspected account takeovers are handled by the Remya Industries Ltd Trust & Safety team, so these cases are always escalated to a human specialist who contacts you within 4 hours.
