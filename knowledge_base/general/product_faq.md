# Remya Industries Ltd Product FAQ (all product lines)

Last updated: 01 October 2026. Product-line details (warranty, sizes, freshness) are in each product line's guide.

## Damaged or defective products

### The product I received is broken or not working. Can I get a replacement?
Yes. Products that arrive damaged or defective are replaced free of charge.
1. Report the problem within 48 hours of delivery for physical damage, or within 10 days of delivery for a manufacturing defect, from My Orders > Report an Issue.
2. Upload photos or a short video showing the damage or the defect, along with the product's serial number if it has one.
3. For large appliances, a technician visit is scheduled within 2 business days to verify the issue. For small appliances such as blenders, mixers, kettles and irons, a free pickup is scheduled within 2 business days.
4. The replacement is dispatched within 3 to 5 business days after the pickup or technician verification.
If the same product is out of stock, you receive a full refund instead.
Grocery items follow the Remya Fresh freshness policy instead of this process.

## Warranty basics

### Does the warranty cover accidental damage?
No. Manufacturer warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage or damage caused by unauthorised repairs. Accidental, liquid and screen damage can be covered by a Remya Shield Device Protection plan.

### Where do I claim warranty after the return window?
Warranty claims after the return window are handled by the brand's authorised service centre. The invoice in My Orders works as proof of purchase.

## Product information

### Are products on Remya Industries Ltd genuine?
All products are sourced directly from brands or their authorised distributors and come with a brand warranty and a GST invoice.
