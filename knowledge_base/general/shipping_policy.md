# Remya Industries Ltd Shipping & Delivery Policy

Applies to all orders placed on the Remya Industries Ltd website and app. Last updated: 01 October 2026.

## Order processing time

### How long does it take to dispatch my order?
Orders placed before 2:00 PM IST on a business day are packed and dispatched the same day. Orders placed after 2:00 PM IST, on Sundays or on public holidays are dispatched on the next business day. You receive a dispatch confirmation with the tracking link by email and SMS as soon as the courier picks up the parcel.

## Delivery timelines

### When will my order arrive?
Standard delivery timelines after dispatch are:
- Metro cities (Chennai, Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune): 2 to 4 business days.
- Other cities and towns: 4 to 7 business days.
- Remote areas and North-East states: 7 to 10 business days.
Express delivery (metro cities only) delivers within 1 business day of dispatch for a fee of Rs. 99.
The expected delivery date is shown on the order page under My Orders.

### Shipping charges
Delivery is free for orders of Rs. 499 or more. Orders below Rs. 499 have a delivery charge of Rs. 49.

## Tracking your order

### Where is my order? How do I track it?
Go to My Orders, select the order and tap Track Package to see the live courier status. The same tracking link is sent in the dispatch email and SMS. Tracking can take up to 12 hours to show the first update after dispatch.

## Late or delayed deliveries

### My order is late. It was supposed to arrive already.
Couriers occasionally miss the expected delivery date because of weather, local holidays or high volumes. If your order has not arrived by the expected delivery date:
1. Check the latest status under My Orders > Track Package.
2. If the order is delayed by up to 2 business days, it is usually delivered within that window and no action is needed.
3. If the order is delayed by more than 2 business days, our support team raises a priority trace with the courier within 24 hours and shares an update with you within 48 hours.
4. If the order is not delivered within 7 days after the expected delivery date, you can choose a full refund to the original payment method or a free reshipment.
Delivery charges paid on a delayed order are refunded automatically when the delay is more than 2 business days.

### The tracking says delivered but I did not receive the package.
Check with neighbours, building security or the reception desk, because couriers sometimes hand parcels to them. If you still cannot find it, report it within 48 hours of the delivered status. We open an investigation with the courier and resolve it within 5 business days with a reshipment or a full refund.

## Damaged, opened or incomplete packages

### My package arrived damaged, torn open or with items missing.
Report the issue within 48 hours of delivery from My Orders > Report an Issue, and upload clear photos of the outer box, the shipping label and the contents. For missing items, we verify the packed weight with the warehouse and resolve the case within 3 business days by sending the missing item or refunding its value. For damaged products, see the Replacement process in the Product FAQ.

## Changing the delivery address

### Can I change my delivery address after placing the order?
The address can be changed from My Orders until the order is dispatched. After dispatch the address cannot be changed, but you can ask the courier to reschedule the delivery date once.
