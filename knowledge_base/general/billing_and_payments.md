# Remya Industries Ltd Billing & Payments

Last updated: 01 October 2026.

## Payment methods

### Which payment methods do you accept?
UPI, credit cards, debit cards, net banking, Remya Industries Ltd Wallet and Cash on Delivery (COD). COD is available for orders up to Rs. 10,000. No-cost EMI is available on credit cards for orders of Rs. 3,000 or more.

## Duplicate or extra charges

### I was charged twice or more for the same order.
If a payment failed and you retried, your bank may show a temporary authorisation hold for the failed attempt. Such holds are reversed automatically by the bank within 5 to 7 business days. If an extra amount has actually been debited and is not reversed after 7 business days, our Billing team investigates the transaction with the payment gateway. Multiple charges, chargebacks and payment disputes are always handled by a human Billing specialist, who contacts the customer within 4 business hours.

## Invoices

### How do I download the invoice for my order?
Open My Orders, select the order and tap Download Invoice. A GST invoice is available once the order is dispatched. For a business GST invoice, add your GSTIN under Account > Business Details before placing the order.

## Promo codes and offers

### My promo code is not working at checkout.
Promo codes do not apply when:
- The code has expired; the expiry date is shown in the offer details.
- The cart value is below the minimum order value for the code.
- The products in the cart are excluded from the offer (for example, items already on a deal price).
- Another promo code is already applied; only one promo code can be used per order.
Codes are case-sensitive, so enter them exactly as shown. If the code is valid and still fails, share a screenshot of the error with support.

## Failed payments

### My payment failed but money was deducted.
If the order was not placed, the deducted amount is refunded automatically to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days. You do not need to raise a request.
