# Remya Industries Ltd Returns & Refund Policy

Last updated: 01 October 2026.

## Return window

### How many days do I have to return a product?
- Footwear, apparel and accessories: 30 days from the delivery date.
- Home and kitchen items (non-electrical): 15 days from the delivery date.
- Electronics and electrical appliances: 10 days from the delivery date, for defective or damaged products only (replacement first, refund if a replacement is not available).
- Remya Fresh grocery: perishable items are not returnable; quality issues are refunded without a return (see the Remya Fresh guide).
Items must be unused, in their original packaging and with all tags, labels, manuals and freebies.

### Which items cannot be returned?
Innerwear, personal care products, perishable food items and personalised or engraved products cannot be returned unless they arrive damaged or defective.

## How to return a product

### How do I return shoes, clothes or other items and get a refund?
Returns are accepted within the return window: 30 days from delivery for footwear, apparel and accessories (see the full list above).
1. Open My Orders and select the item.
2. Tap Return, choose the reason and select Refund or Exchange.
3. A free pickup is scheduled within 2 business days. Keep the item packed with its tags and original box.
4. After pickup, the item goes through a quality check within 2 business days.
5. Once the quality check is passed, the refund is initiated.
There is no return shipping charge for returns made within the return window.

### Can I exchange shoes or clothes for a different size?
Yes. Choose Exchange instead of Refund on the return screen and select the new size. The replacement size is dispatched when the original item is picked up, subject to stock.

## Refund timelines

### How long does a refund take?
After the refund is initiated:
- UPI and Remya Industries Ltd Wallet: 1 to 3 business days.
- Credit and debit cards: 5 to 7 business days.
- Net banking: 5 to 7 business days.
- Cash on Delivery orders: refunded to the bank account or UPI ID you provide, within 5 to 7 business days.
You receive an email and SMS with the refund reference number when the refund is initiated. If the refund has not reached you after the stated time, share the refund reference number with your bank first, then contact support.

## Cancellations

### Can I cancel my order?
Orders can be cancelled free of charge from My Orders before they are dispatched. Prepaid cancellations are refunded within the refund timelines above. Once dispatched, the order cannot be cancelled but can be returned after delivery under this policy.
