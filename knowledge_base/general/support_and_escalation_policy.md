# Remya Industries Ltd Customer Support & Escalation Policy

Last updated: 01 October 2026.

## Support channels and hours

### How can I contact Remya Industries Ltd support?
Customers can reach Remya Industries Ltd Customer Support through email (support@remyaindustries.example), WhatsApp, Telegram and the Help section of the app. AI-assisted support is available 24x7. Human specialists are available from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM IST, all days of the week.

## Response time commitments

### How quickly will support respond?
- High priority (payment disputes, fraud or account takeover, safety issues, legal notices): first response from a human specialist within 2 hours during support hours.
- Medium priority (late deliveries, damaged products, refunds not received, login problems): response within 8 hours.
- Low priority (general questions and information requests): response within 24 hours.

## When a case goes to a human specialist

### Which cases are escalated to a human?
A ticket is escalated to a human specialist when any of these apply:
- The customer reports fraud, an unauthorised transaction or an account takeover.
- The case involves a payment dispute, multiple charges or a chargeback.
- The customer mentions legal action, a lawyer, a consumer court or the police.
- The customer is very upset or the issue is urgent.
- The question is not covered by Remya Industries Ltd's published policies.
- The automated answer is not confident enough.
When a ticket is escalated, the customer receives the ticket number and a specialist continues the conversation on the same channel.

### Who handles my escalated ticket?
Escalated tickets go to the specialist desk for the product line or service involved: Electronics Desk, Appliances Desk, Fashion Desk, Remya Fresh Grocery Desk, Remya Care Services Desk, Remya Shield Claims Desk or Plus Membership Desk. Fraud and account takeover cases go to the Trust & Safety team, payment disputes to the Billing Desk, and legal notices to Customer Relations.

## Feedback

### How do I give feedback about support?
After a ticket is closed, the customer receives a short survey link. Feedback is reviewed weekly by the support quality team.
